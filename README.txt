CMPM-120 Project 3 Introduction
By: Vincent Dang, Tianyunran Huang

Goals:
Complete all levels by control the character to reach the exits. Be aware of the danger and terrain in the maps. Try find the best path to finish it as fast as you can and collect all the coins.

Controls:
Move: A / D or Left / Right
Jump: Space
Interact/Climb: W / Up
Drop:: S / Down

Camera: Follow the player movements and only show parts of the maps in each level.

Level 1:Vincent Dang
Level 2:Tianyunran Huang

Level Select:
Press M in game to open the menu page, and easily select the level by click the button.

Bonus:
Complex Camera: The camera will not out of the view and will move up and down depending where's the player in the map.
Ways to die: In level 2, Player will die and starts from the beginning of the level if touch the hazards.
Puzzles: In level 1, player have multiple ways to reach the level exits. And player needs to think different paths if they want to collect all the coins. Conveyor-belt surfaces also affect player movement.
